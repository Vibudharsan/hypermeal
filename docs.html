Endpoints

MethodPathPurpose
GET/api/healthProvider configuration status
GET/api/foods?q=riceOffline illustrative food catalog
GET/api/products/{barcode}Open Food Facts label values
POST/api/analyzeBase64 photo → editable food suggestions
POST/api/estimateItems and grams → nutrition totals
GET / POST/api/mealsList or save meals
GET / PUT / DELETE/api/meals/{id}Read, replace or delete an entry
GET/api/summary?date=YYYY-MM-DDDaily totals
GET/api/exportExport diary as JSON

Calories are kcal. Protein, carbohydrates and fat are grams. Custom nutrients use a 100g basis. Estimates are not medical or dietary diagnoses.
